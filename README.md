# Dr.sorted: Health App for Smaller Indian Cities
An early-stage health app that brings testing, results, and daily guidance into one place for people who live far from labs.
## How it works
1. Take a basic symptom check in the app
2. Book a home test, where a technician comes to your door
3. Get your report in the app, explained in simple language
4. Receive a personalized diet plan and follow-up suggestions

## Planned open-source modules
- Symptom-check logic
- Lab report explainer

## Later phase
A smart toilet-seat device that tracks urine and stool health, sends a daily report to the app, and flags unusual changes with a suggestion to see a doctor.

## Status
Early stage. Nothing is built yet, and I'm starting development now.
